Statement and definitions

The formal theorems

An extriangulated category is given by a bifunctor 𝔼 with values in k-modules and a realization predicate satisfying the axioms of Nakaoka and Palu. An extriangulated functor into a pretriangulated category sends realizations to distinguished triangles through a natural transformation η; it is a triangulated embedding when it is fully faithful and the target is triangulated, and an algebraic realization when moreover η is bijective and the target is algebraic. Heredity is the vanishing of the tensor powers 𝔼n, n ≥ 2, of Gorsky, Nakaoka and Palu. The category 𝒞(D) is Mathlib's additive envelope of the labels X, Y, Ai, Pi, with 𝔼 = Ext1 in the representations of I with a least point adjoined.

ExtriangulatedRealization.theoremA, theoremB, theoremB_necessity, proposition_2_3, corollary_2_4, proposition_4_4 and proposition_4_6 prove these statements.
